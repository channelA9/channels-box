This paper presents a counterfactual testing framework to evaluate the faithfulness of AI-generated explanations for predictive machine learning models. Our findings reveal that LLMs exhibit a strong cognitive bias towards plausibility over faithfulness, highlighting critical challenges for developing trustworthy, human-centered AI systems.
