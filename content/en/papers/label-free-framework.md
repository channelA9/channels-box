This paper conducted an analysis of a year's worth of data from our university's RAG chatbot with no ground-truth labels. The goal was to use scalable and automated methods like cosine similarity (groundedness, relevance) alongside reward models (usefulness) to see if they can be used to detect patterns in large-scale production data without human annotation.
