It’s been fun, Vancouver!

I recently completed my research internship at Simon Fraser University at the VVISE Lab under Dr. Wolfgang Stuerzlinger. This has been an incredible opportunity and I’m really grateful for the opportunity to work at an HCI lab for the first time and also get to meet so many incredible people. 

I worked on a project involving visualization systems for AI hallucination detection. It was my first time conducting user studies and my first dip into more direct HCI research. We're hoping to have a publication ready from the work soon.

Beyond the lab, I had a lot of fun exploring Vancouver and the surrounding areas. Vancouver really felt like a mix between Los Angeles and Tokyo, with its beautiful mountains and beaches but also a bustling city center with great food and a great diversity of people. The environment also motivated me to buy my first camera! 

I'm really going to miss the SkyTrain. And the cooler weather. And the green scenery.

On the other hand, it's also nice to be back home and catching up with everyone!

Until next time, Canada!
